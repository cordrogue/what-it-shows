The problems

parser · the shared parser reads dates month first (ticket). Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The parser reads 07/04 as July 4th, so a booking for 7 April goes out on 4 July. Only dates with day and month both 12 or less can swap, which is why it looks random to the people who report it. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.

hint · booking-v3 tells day-first people to type the month first (ticket). The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Its tenants (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) write the day first. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. This is not the parser: fixing the parser leaves it as it is. Fix: make the hint follow the tenant’s locale.

shift · dates show one day early (ticket). These reports are not a swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A calendar date is converted through a time zone and lands on the day before. The desk had already noted these as one-day moves; the old table still counts them as parser. One reporter says they stopped reporting these, so the real number is higher. Fix: keep booking dates date-only end to end.

Why intake’s tags do not count

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser the moment it arrives. The cause depends on the tenant and the form they run. The table above shows how often intake’s tag matches the problem. Count from the tenant, not from the tag.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs that look alike. Before you merge, ask what fix closes each report.
  • Same symptom, same subject line, same ticket does not mean same cause (decision 0001 merges on subject alone).
